001 / Time & Rooms

The Clock

They never stop running

Arrows showing the passing moment

They never truly touch

Like lovers always missing each other

They draw the circle of time

Beginning and end joy and sorrow

They are the masters of our lives

The silent witnesses of our days

And every tick-tock that resonates

Is a small death a small birth

The rhythm of the universe

In the small space of the house.
