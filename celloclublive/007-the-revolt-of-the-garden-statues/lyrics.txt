The garden gnome said
I am tired of smiling like an idiot with my wheelbarrow
the plaster Venus said
I am cold and I would like a coat
the pink plastic flamingo said
I would like to see the ocean for real
So they left the garden
The gnome went to work in a coal mine
Venus signed up with a modeling agency
the pink flamingo bought a plane ticket to the tropics
The owner of the garden was very angry
he said
Where is the respect for property
He replaced the statues
with security cameras
and his garden became very safe
and very sad.
