The period said
I am tired of ending everything
the comma said
I am weary of making pauses
the exclamation mark said
Enough with the shouting
the question mark said
I no longer want to doubt
So they all resigned
The sentences became endless rivers
without breath without emotion without a question
a long river of gray words
People stopped reading
it was too boring
They understood that punctuation
is not decoration
it is the breath of the text
it is its music its heart
and they begged forgiveness
from the modest little signs
that give the language
all of its life.
