Their goal is for everyone to be the same
same house same car same dog
same ideas same dreams same fears
They distribute manuals on "how to live"
they organize conformity championships
they punish originality
Their slogan is
"Difference is a disease"
They have almost succeeded
The world has become a giant mirror
where everyone sees their own reflection everywhere
But one day a child laughed
with a laugh that was not in the manual
a free and wild laugh
and that laughter made a crack in the giant mirror
and through the crack
you could begin to see
a little bit of blue sky.
