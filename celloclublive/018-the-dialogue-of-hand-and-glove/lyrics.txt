The hand said to the glove
You protect me from the cold
but you stop me from feeling
The glove replied
I protect you from the dirt
but I stop you from touching
The hand said
Without you I am vulnerable
The glove replied
Without you I am nothing but an empty piece of leather
So they decided never to leave each other
The hand agreed to be a little less sensitive
the glove agreed to be a little less clean
and together they faced the world
one giving warmth
the other giving life
A perfect love story
made of compromise
and necessity.
