I was pink and full of sugar
in the mouth of a young girl
I knew the taste of her kisses
then she threw me on the sidewalk
I became gray and hard
stuck to the asphalt
shoes crush me
the rain washes me
I am the sticky memory of a fleeting pleasure
I am the small scar of passing time
I am no longer useful for anything
except perhaps to trip up
a man in a hurry
so that he stops for a second
and looks at the sky
and remembers
the sweet taste of his own youth.
