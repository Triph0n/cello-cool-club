He entered her room
he did not take off his coat
he sat on the bed
and asked her
Tell me about yourself
She was surprised
no one ever asked her that
She spoke of her childhood
of her dreams of her fears
He listened without saying a word
for an hour
Then he put the money on the table
and left
She looked at the money
then she looked at herself in the mirror
and for the first time in a long time
she did not see a prostitute
she saw a woman
and she understood that this man
had not bought her body
he had returned to her
her soul.
