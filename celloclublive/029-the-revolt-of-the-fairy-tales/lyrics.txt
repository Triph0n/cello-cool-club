Little Red Riding Hood said
I am tired of the wolf I want a prince charming
Cinderella said
Enough with the prince I want to start a union for scullery maids
Sleeping Beauty woke up all by herself
and said
A hundred years of sleep is enough I want a coffee and a newspaper
Prince Charming was unemployed
the wolf became a vegetarian
the wicked queen opened a beauty salon
Everything was upside down
the children understood nothing anymore
but they found it much more amusing
because the characters had finally decided
to no longer be the prisoners
of their own story.
