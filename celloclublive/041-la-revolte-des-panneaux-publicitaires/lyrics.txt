The billboard for the car said
Buy freedom but stay in traffic jams
The billboard for the perfume said
Be irresistible but stay alone in front of your mirror
The billboard for the bank said
Realize your dreams by going into debt for thirty years
One day they got tired of lying
They tore down their own posters
And they showed the gray wall behind
The bare and honest wall
And they wrote on it with broken neon letters
LIFE
IS
ELSE
WHERE
And people looked up from their phones
And for the first time
They saw the sky.
