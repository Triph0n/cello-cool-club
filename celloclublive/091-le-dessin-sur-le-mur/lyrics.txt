The wall was gray and sad
A wall of prison or hospital
A wall that expected nothing
A child passed by
With a piece of white chalk
He drew a door
Then he drew a key
Then he drew a path
Starting from the door and climbing toward the sky
The wall said nothing
But it felt something stir within itself
A crack of light
A desire to open
The child left
But the door remained
And at night the prisoners and the sick
Dream that they take the key
And set off on the path
Leading to the land where walls
Do not exist.
