I have a scar on my hand
It is not a war wound
It is not a dog bite
It is the memory of a shard of glass
The day you broke a vase
Because I had said one word too many
The blood flowed
You cried
You took my hand in yours
And you kissed the wound
The wound healed
But the scar remained
It is white and thin
It is the map of our love
A map that shows the exact place
Where passion almost destroyed everything
And where forgiveness
Saved everything.
