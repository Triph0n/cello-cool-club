The rain said to the window
Why are you so cold and so hard
The window replied
Why are you so sad and so wet
The rain said
I weep for the sorrow of the world
The window said
Me, I reflect its solitude
The rain said
I would like to pass through you to comfort the people
The window said
Me, I would like to break to let them out
So they made a pact
The rain struck so hard
That the window shattered into a thousand pieces
And the rain entered the house
And the people went out into the street
And for the first time
They danced together in the rain
Free and wet.
