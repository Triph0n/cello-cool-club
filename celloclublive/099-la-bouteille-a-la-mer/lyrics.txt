I wrote a poem
On a little scrap of paper
A poem that said
I am alone
Love me
I put it in an empty bottle
And threw it into the sea
The sea carried it away
For years it traveled
It saw whales and storms
One day it washed up on a deserted beach
Nobody found it
The sun erased the ink
The paper turned back to white
The bottle remained there
Empty
Like the heart of the one who had cast it
Proving that hope
Is the most beautiful form
Of despair.
