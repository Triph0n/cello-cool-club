The question mark had had enough
Enough of always asking questions
Enough of always doubting
Enough of being the symbol of uncertainty
One day it stood up straight
It lost its curve
It became an exclamation mark
It began to assert things
The sky is green
The fish sing
Love is simple
It was wonderful
The world was full of joyful certitudes
But people began to get bored
There was no more mystery
Nothing left to discover
So the exclamation mark regretted its curve
It understood that asking a question
Is often more beautiful
Than knowing the answer
And it gently bent back again
Happy to be once more
A fishhook to catch dreams.
