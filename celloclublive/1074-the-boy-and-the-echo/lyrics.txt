A boy stands at the edge of the valley
small against the mountains
and the mountains are big
and say nothing
And because he was scolded that morning
and because he is seven
and the world is unfair
he takes a breath
and shouts into the valley
YOU IDIOT
And the valley takes a breath
and gives it back
idiot idiot idiot
three times four times
once from every side
The boy sits down in the grass
and cries
grandpa the mountains don't like me
they call me names
did you hear?
Grandpa sits down beside him
pipe smoke rising straight
like a proper upbringing
Try telling them something else
he says
mountains repeat
they can't make anything up
they are only big
The boy stands up
wipes his nose on his sleeve
breathes in down to his boots
and shouts
I LOVE YOU
And the valley gives it back
I love you I love you I love you
once from every side
and then once more
and it keeps on saying it
long into the evening
the world is one big echo
it gives you back
whatever you call into it
