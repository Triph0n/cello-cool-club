A poem escaped from a book
It was completely naked without a title without an author
It wandered through the streets
It knocked on the doors of hearts
But the hearts were closed
Or busy with something else
The poem grew cold
It sat down on a bench
It began to weep words of rain
An old lady passed by
She picked it up
She put it in her pocket
Against her heart
She did not read it
She just kept it warm
And the poem understood
That it had no need to be read
In order to exist
It only needed
A little human warmth.
