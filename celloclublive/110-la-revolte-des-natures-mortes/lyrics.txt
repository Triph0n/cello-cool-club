In the museum at night
The apple of
Cézanne said
I am fed up with being an apple
I want to roll on the ground
The violin of
Picasso said
I want to play some wild music
Not be a wooden jigsaw puzzle
The pipe of
Magritte said
This is a pipe and I want to smoke
So all the still lifes came to life
The fruits organized a food fight
The instruments played a cacophonous symphony
The objects danced on the walls
The night watchman thought he was going mad
He called the police
But when the police arrived
Everything had become calm and motionless again
Everything in its place
Except that the apple of
Cézanne
Had a tiny bite
And that the pipe of
Magritte
Still smelled a little of stale tobacco.
