The table said
I am fed up with bearing the weight of meals and elbows
The chair said
Enough of supporting the weight of backsides and troubles
The bed said
I am tired of restless sleep and sad loves
The wardrobe said
I no longer want to keep skeletons and old secrets
So they moved out all on their own
The table left for the countryside to become a picnic table
The chair went to the theater to listen to stories
The bed settled on a beach to watch the stars
And the wardrobe opened wide in a forest
So that birds could build their nests in it
The apartment was empty
And the man who lived there
Had to learn to sit on the floor
And to sleep standing up
And to live without owning anything
And he found that it was
Much lighter.
