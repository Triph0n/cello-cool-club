The hammer said
More strength is needed
The screwdriver said
More precision is needed
The saw said
One must know how to cut
The tape measure said
Everything must be measured
They talked all night long
Each praising its own qualities
Then the nail spoke up
A tiny nail
It said
You are all very useful
But without me
You are useless
For it is I who hold things together
I am the bond the goal the final end
The tools fell silent
They understood that strength precision measurement
Are nothing
Without a little nail of love
To join everything together.
