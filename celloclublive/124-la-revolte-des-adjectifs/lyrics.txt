The adjective "beautiful" said
I am fed up with qualifying sunsets and women
I want to qualify a just action
The adjective "great" said
Enough of qualifying generals and bankers
I want to qualify a small act of kindness
The adjective "true" said
I refuse to let myself be coupled with the word "lie"
So they left their nouns
They floated freely in the air
And they settled where they wanted "Beautiful" settled on the gesture of a worker sharing his bread "Great" settled on the silence of a man who listens "True" settled on a child's tear
The world became more just
And more difficult to describe
For beauty and greatness
Were no longer where we were used
To looking for them.
