Saint
Christopher said

Saint
Rita patron saint of hopeless causes said
My own cause is hopeless I resign
So all the saints left their churches
They went to have a drink at the café opposite
They talked about their lives of sacrifices
Of their miracles and their doubts
People entered the empty churches
They prayed in the silence
They prayed not to saints
But to their own hopes their own strengths
And for the first time
Some prayers
Were answered.
