He had a poem tattooed on his heart
A poem that no one could read
Except the woman he loved
She would lay her head on his chest
And she would read the words with her fingers
With her lips
It was their secret their garden
One day the woman left
The poem remained
But it had become illegible
The letters had jumbled up
The words no longer made sense
It was no longer a love poem
It was a scar
A confused map of pain
And the man spent the rest of his life
Trying to decipher
The language of his own sorrow.
