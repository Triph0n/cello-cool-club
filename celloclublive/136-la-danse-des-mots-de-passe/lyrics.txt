My first password was my dog's name
My second the name of my first love
My third a birth date
Now my passwords are monsters "Zk78!bX_p"
Soulless combinations
That protect my digital life
My bank my emails my photos
I am a prisoner of my own secrets
I spend my time inventing passwords
Forgetting them
Resetting them
I have a fortress around my life
But inside there is not much left
Just a man who is afraid
And who has forgotten the password
The most important one
The one that opens the door
Of his own heart.
