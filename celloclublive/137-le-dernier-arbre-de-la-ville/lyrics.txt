It was the last tree
An old chestnut tree on a concrete square
Birds no longer dared to perch there
For fear of being the only ones
Lovers no longer carved their hearts on it
For fear it would bring bad luck
The tree was alone
It watched the cars the buildings the noise
It remembered the time when there was a forest
Animals rivers
It was the last witness of a vanished world
One day men came with saws
They said
It is dangerous it is going to fall
They cut it down
And in its place they put
A bronze statue
Representing a tree
And everyone found it
Very modern
And very safe.
