On the
Pont des
Arts there is a man
Who sells neither padlocks nor portraits
He sells memories by calling out
A kilo of first love for ten francs
A very ripe regret a little bitter
A handful of children's laughter guaranteed fresh
He has second-hand hopes in good condition
Comfortable silences for two people
Blind rages almost brand new
A tear of joy in a small glass vial
The police pass by and say nothing
Memories are not subject to
VAT
A young woman approaches
I would like to forget she says
The seller shakes his head
Sorry madam I don't do buybacks
I only sell what people have lost
Not what they would like to throw away.
