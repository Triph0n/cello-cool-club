One Tuesday morning at exactly eight o'clock
All the clocks in the town stopped
The one at the station the one at the church the one at the post office
The wristwatches the alarm clocks the Swiss cuckoo clocks
They had had enough of slicing life into pieces
Enough of running after a future that never arrives
Enough of eating seconds like dry insects
The bosses shouted
The teachers screamed
The generals stamped their feet
But time moved no longer
People went out into the streets
They looked at each other for the first time
They talked
They had a coffee that lasted for hours
Children played on the highways
It was the most beautiful day in the world
A day that never ended
For no one ever knew what time it was to end it.
