The new teacher said
Today a lesson in silence
He wrote
SILENCE on the blackboard
And he sat down
The children had never seen anything like it
At first they whispered
Then they grew quiet
And in the silence they began to hear
The sound of their own beating hearts
The sound of a fly bumping against the windowpane
The sound of a cloud drifting across the sky
The sound of an idea born in a neighbor's head
Silence was not empty
It was full of small secret tunes
The teacher smiled
He erased the word
SILENCE
And wrote instead
LISTEN
The bell rang
But for the first time
Nobody wanted to go out.
