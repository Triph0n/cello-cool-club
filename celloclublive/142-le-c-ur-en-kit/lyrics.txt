I bought my heart in a department store
It was a flat-pack kit in a cardboard box
With a very complicated instruction manual
Part
A: tenderness
Part
B: passion (handle with care)
Part
C: jealousy (manipulate with gloves)
Screws of trust and nuts of patience
I assembled it all as I was supposed to
But there was always one part left over
A strange little part without a name
I put it aside
My heart worked well
It loved it suffered it hoped
But one day it stopped
The repairman came
He looked at my heart and said
Ah but the most important part is missing
The strange little part without a name
It is the part of forgiveness
Without it no heart can ever truly restart.
