The accountant said to the poet
You are not profitable
Your words bring in nothing
Your dreams pay no taxes
The poet looked at the accountant
And he said
And you what do you do
The accountant replied proudly
I count
I count other people's money
I put life into columns
Debit
Credit
The poet smiled
I count too
I count the stars in the sky
I count the tears on a cheek
I count the beats of a heart in love
And at the end of the day
It is I who am the richest
For these numbers of mine
Can never be stolen.
