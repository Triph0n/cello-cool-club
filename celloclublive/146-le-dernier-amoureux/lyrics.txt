In a world of faces lit by screens
In a world of conversations made of thumbs up
There was a man and a woman
Who met in a library
They did not exchange profiles
They exchanged books
Then they sat on a bench
And they talked
With their mouths their eyes their hands
They talked about the weather
About the color of the sky and the taste of coffee
They did not take a photo
They made a memory
From the fabric of the present moment
People walked by and looked at them
Like animals of an extinct species
Strange creatures from another time
The man and the woman smiled
They were the last lovers
The only survivors
Of the great digital shipwreck.
