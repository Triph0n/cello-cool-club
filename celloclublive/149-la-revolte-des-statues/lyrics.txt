One night the statues had had enough
The general on his bronze horse
The poet with his stone pen
The king with his marble sceptre
Sick of standing in the rain and the sun
Sick of being a perch for pigeons
Sick of representing a dead glory
So they stepped down from their pedestals
The general charged the cars
The poet wrote insults on the walls of banks
The king tried to sell his crown for a coffee
It was a great night of disorder and freedom
In the morning the police arrested them all
For vandalism of public property
And put them back on their pedestals
But something had changed
In their stone eyes
There was now a tiny spark of defiance
And they were just waiting
For the next night.
