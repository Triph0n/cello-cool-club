There was a very sad man
Who hated colors
One day he built a large machine
A color vacuum cleaner
He sucked up the red of the poppies
The blue of the sky
The green of the trees
The yellow of the sun
The world became grey
Grey like his heart
People were sad and walked with heads bowed
But a child had kept a small box of colored pencils
He sat on the ground
And began to draw
A big yellow sun a red flower a blue sky
The colors were so bright so strong
That they blew up the thief's machine
And the world recovered its colors
And even the thief
Got a little spot of red on the tip of his grey nose.
