I am an inkblot on a white page
I am not a mistake
I am a possibility
I can be a black bird taking flight
A hole in reality to peek through to the other side
The shadow of a word that didn't dare write itself
The eye of a gentle monster
The poet looks at me
He doesn't reach for his eraser
He reaches for his pen
And around me he draws a world
He draws mountains rivers houses
And suddenly I am no longer a blot
I am the lake in the middle of the landscape
I am the black and deep heart of his poem
And I am happy
For even a mistake
Can become the center of the universe.
