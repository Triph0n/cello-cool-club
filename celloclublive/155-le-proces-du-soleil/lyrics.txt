One day they put on trial the
Sun
The prosecution said
It is too hot it causes heatstroke
It is too bright it hurts the eyes
It reveals the dust on the furniture
And the wrinkles on the faces
It is guilty of too much truth
The defense called a witness
A sunflower
The sunflower said nothing
It just turned its head slowly toward the
Sun
And smiled
The judge looked at the sunflower
Then he looked at the
Sun
And he said
The accused is acquitted
Those who do not like the light
Can just stay in their cellar
The court is adjourned
And everyone went to have a drink on the terrace.
