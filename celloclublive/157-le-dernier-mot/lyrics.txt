The old man was on his deathbed
His family was around him
The priest spoke to him of paradise
The doctor spoke to him of morphine
Everyone was waiting for his last word
The word that would sum up his life
The word of wisdom for posterity
The old man opened his eyes
He looked at his wife his son his daughter his dog
He looked at the priest and the doctor
He took a deep breath
And he said
Damn
Then he died
The family was shocked
The priest was scandalized
The doctor noted the time of death
Only the dog understood
He wagged his tail
For 'Damn' was the word the old man said
When he was truly happy
And couldn't find a word big enough to say it.
