By dint of taking the same bus at the same time
Sitting at the same desk in front of the same wall
Eating the same sandwich at the same break
The man became a statue
One morning he did not get up
He remained sitting on the square's bench
Motionless and grey
Pigeons poop on his head
Lovers carve their names on his back
Children play hide-and-seek behind him
He feels nothing
He has become a monument to boredom
Sometimes a stone tear flows down his cheek
When he remembers a time
When he was made of flesh and blood
And when he still knew how to run.
