One day the words had had enough
The word
LOVE no longer wanted to be in the mouths of tyrants
The word
FREEDOM no longer wanted to be on prison walls
The word
TRUTH was tired of lying
So they went on strike
They marched into the streets
The politician opened his mouth for a speech
And nothing came out
The general wanted to shout an order
And made the sound of a duck
The advertiser wanted to sell soap
And only stammered
It was a great silence
A magnificent and terrible silence
And in this silence
We finally heard the sound of the wind in the trees
And the beating heart of a sleeping child.
