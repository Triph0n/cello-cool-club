In the city of concrete and steel
One tree remained
An old plane tree in a small square
Developers wanted to chop it down
To build a parking lot
But the people of the neighborhood said no
Children formed a ring around it
Lovers carved their hearts on its bark
The old folks came to sit in its shade
The tree was no longer a tree
It was the heart of the neighborhood
So the developers built their parking lot elsewhere
And the tree remained
It gives neither fruit nor flowers
It gives only shade and hope
And that is much more important.
