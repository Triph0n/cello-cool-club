In a large factory near the sky
Workers in grey coats manufacture the clouds
There is the cumulus department
For lazy afternoons
The stratus department
For melancholy mornings
And the nimbus department
For when the world needs washing
The foreman shouts
Faster more clouds
The boss wants rain on the competitor's shares
But a young dreamy worker
Secretly makes clouds in the shape of a horse
Of a face or a boat
He releases them into the sky
And watches the children below
Who point at them laughing
It is his small act of poetic sabotage.
