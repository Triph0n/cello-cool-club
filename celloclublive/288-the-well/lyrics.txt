In the middle of the square stands a well
and on it hangs a sign
WATER NOT DRINKABLE
and across the bucket a grate
and across the grate a lock
the council closed it
with a stamp and a by-law
the well is forbidden
the well is dangerous
the well is officially dead
but people still come
and slip their fingers through the grate
and drop a coin down
and over this water no one may drink
they whisper their wishes
let him come back
let her get well
let him say yes to me
let the children write from abroad
let this at last pass over
the coins fall into the dark
they chime on the stone and on each other
a small rain of copper and silver
wish after wish year after year
at the bottom a whole town piles up
and one day the council comes
with a stamp and a pail
to remove that improper water
and hauls up the bucket full
of coins instead of water
and counts them from the bottom up
and finds there is just enough
exactly enough to mend the well
so it pays for a new pump
out of the wishes it had banned
a wish cannot be forbidden
not by a sign nor by a lock
it falls into the dark patiently
and in the end
it saves itself
