The Dialogue of Hammer and Glass
(engine card 040, poem/poster #080)

The hammer said to the glass
I am strength I am power
I can break you into a thousand pieces
The glass answered nothing
It just let the light pass through
It reflected the sky
The face of the child who was watching
The hammer struck
The glass shattered into a million shards of light
Each shard continued to reflect the sky
The hammer remained there
Heavy and stupid in its victory
Having succeeded in breaking
Only beauty
Without ever understanding it.
