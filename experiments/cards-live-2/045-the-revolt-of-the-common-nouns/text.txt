The Revolt of the Common Nouns
(engine card 045, poem/poster #082)

The table said
I no longer want to be a table I want to be a mountain
The chair said
I no longer want to be a chair I want to be a bird
The window said
I no longer want to be a window I want to be the horizon
So they exchanged their names
The table became a mountain where cats went mountaineering
The chair became a bird that flew away over the mountain
The window became the horizon where the bird vanished
The man in the room was left alone
With words that no longer meant anything
And he understood that poetry
Is when you give a chair
Permission
To fly away.
