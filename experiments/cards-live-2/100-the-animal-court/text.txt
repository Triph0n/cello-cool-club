The Animal Court
(engine card 100, poem/poster #088)

One day the animals put man on trial
The lion said
He kills for pleasure not for hunger
The bird said
He cages the sky
The horse said
He whips me when I am tired
The lamb said
He eats me while talking of peace
The tree said
He cuts me down to print his stupid laws
The river said
He turns me into a dustbin
So all the animals condemned man
To the worst of punishments
Solitude
They left him alone on the earth
With his money his machines and his intelligence
And man looked around him
And for the first time
He was afraid
Of his own victory.
