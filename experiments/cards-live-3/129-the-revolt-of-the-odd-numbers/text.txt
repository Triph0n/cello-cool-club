The Revolt of the Odd Numbers
(engine card 129, poem/poster #095)

The
One the
Three the
Five the
Seven the
Nine
Had had enough
Enough of being odd
Enough of not being able to be divided in two
Enough of being the symbols of solitude and imbalance
They decided to unite
They formed a large strange and magnificent number
A number that meant nothing
A number that obeyed no logic
The even numbers looked at them with contempt
You are not rational they said
The odd numbers replied
So what
Life is not rational
Love is not rational
Poetry is not rational
We are the numbers of life
And we are proud
To be a little twisted.
