The Merchant of First Words
(engine card 130, poem/poster #096)

He sells the first words of children
In small music boxes "Mama" "Papa" "Again"
Every word has the music of the voice that spoke it
A pure music full of wonder
Old people come to buy them from him
When their memory fades
They open a box
And they hear the first word of their child
Who is now fifty years old
And who no longer talks to them
It is a sweet little torture
A small happiness that hurts
The merchant is a sad man
He sells the beginnings of stories
But he knows that all stories
End one day
In silence.
