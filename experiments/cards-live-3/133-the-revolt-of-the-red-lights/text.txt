The Revolt of the Red Lights
(engine card 133, poem/poster #099)

The red light said
I am fed up with being the symbol of forbidden things
The amber light said
I am tired of being the symbol of hesitation
So they exchanged their roles
The red light became green
The green light became amber
The amber light became red
The motorists went mad
They speeded up at red
They braked at green
They honked at amber
It was chaos
But a joyful chaos
A chaos where rules no longer existed
Where everyone had to trust
Their own judgment
And that of others
And that day
Nobody arrived late
For the destination
Was no longer the most important thing.
