The Thief of Voices
(engine card 014, poem/poster #073)

He steals people's voices
he captures them in a tape recorder
He has the broken voice of the old man
the clear voice of the young girl
the loud voice of the politician
the soft voice of the mother
At night he listens to his collection
he gets drunk on all these lives
He is a thousand people at once
but he is no one
because he lost his own voice
a long time ago
trying to scream too loudly
against the injustice of the world
Now he is mute
and he has only the voices of others left
to remember
that he once existed.
