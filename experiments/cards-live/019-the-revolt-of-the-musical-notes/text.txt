The Revolt of the Musical Notes
(engine card 019, poem/poster #075)

The C note said
I am tired of always being the beginning
the B note said
I am weary of always being the end
the Sharp said
I want to be natural for once
the Flat said
I would like to go up a little
So they left the sheet music
they flew around the room
they created a new kind of music
a music without rules without a conductor
a free and wild music
The pianist tried to catch them
but his fingers were too slow too disciplined
He had to stop and listen
and he understood that the most beautiful music
is not the one that is written down
but the one that invents itself
in every moment.
