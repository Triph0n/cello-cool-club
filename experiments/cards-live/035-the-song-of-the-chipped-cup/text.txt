The Song of the Chipped Cup
(engine card 035, poem/poster #079)

I am a chipped cup
They put me at the very back of the cupboard
no one wants to drink from me anymore
I have a little wound of porcelain
a souvenir from a clumsy hand
or from an anger too sharp
I am no longer perfect
but I am far more interesting
My chip is a little mouth
that could tell so many stories
the story of coffee served too hot
of tears that dripped inside
of kisses whispered over my brim
I am a cup with a past
and that is so much better
than a brand new cup
which has as of yet
nothing to say.
