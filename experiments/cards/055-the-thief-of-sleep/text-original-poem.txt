The Thief of Sleep
(engine card 055, poem #069)

He does not steal money
He steals sleep
At night he enters bedrooms
Without making a sound
And he takes people's sleep
He puts it in a large bag
In the morning people wake up tired
With dark circles under their eyes
They do not know their rest was stolen from them

The thief goes home
And he gives the stolen sleep
To his sick little girl
Who cannot sleep because of the pain
She finally falls asleep
With a smile

And the thief watches her
With a heavy heart
To be at once
A criminal and a savior.
