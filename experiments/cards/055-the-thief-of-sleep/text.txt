The Thief of Sleep
(engine card 055, poem #069)

He does not steal money
he steals sleep
At night he slips into bedrooms
making no sound at all
and takes the sleep from people
stuffing it into a great sack
In the morning people wake up weary
with dark shadows under their eyes
they do not know their rest has been stolen

The thief goes home
and gives the stolen sleep
to his little sick daughter
who cannot rest because of the pain
She drifts off at last
with a smile

And the thief watches her
his heart heavy
with being both
a criminal and a savior
