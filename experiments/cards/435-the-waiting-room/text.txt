The Waiting Room
(engine card 435)

The doctor's waiting room
smells of iodine and old paper
three coats on the coat rack
that have never met
A man with his arm in a sling
a girl coughing into her sleeve
an old lady with a shopping bag
sitting exactly one chair apart
as politeness requires
Magazines on the table
the princess is getting married
the princess divorced long ago
the horoscope says what to do in August
which August it was
nobody knows anymore
a recipe for perfect Christmas cookies
from a Christmas long since digested
Nobody speaks
and yet they understand each other
one cough answers another
one sigh another sigh
it is a shared silence
like a blanket over the knees
Next, calls the nurse
You go first, the old lady tells the man with the arm
I'm in no hurry
and then to the girl
go on, dear, you have school
I have all the time in the world
When the waiting room is empty
the doctor comes to the door
Mrs Smith, nothing wrong again?
Nothing, doctor
at home the silence is only mine
here for a while it was ours
