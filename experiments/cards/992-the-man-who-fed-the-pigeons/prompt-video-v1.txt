poster-video-v1.png (= assets/posters/001-the-man-who-fed-the-pigeons.png) predates the
practice of saving the generation prompt alongside each poster — the original prompt
text was never captured and is not recoverable. The file itself is the first-generation
narrative scene (old man on a bench covered in pigeons, forbidding sign, fountain, the
clerk who signed the sign) and is the actual poster_path used to build the pilot video
(see experiments/poster-video-001/manifest.json).
