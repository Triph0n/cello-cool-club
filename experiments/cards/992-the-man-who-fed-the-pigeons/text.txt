The Man Who Fed the Pigeons
(engine card 992, poem/poster #001)

Every morning at seven
on the market square
an old man in a grey coat
crumbled bread for the birds

and the birds knew him
the way one knows church bells

Then came the sign
FEEDING BIRDS FORBIDDEN
fine one thousand francs
signature illegible

Next morning at seven
the old man came
sat down on the bench
with empty hands
entirely empty

and the birds came all the same
landed on his shoulders
on his hat
on his knees

The clerk who had signed the sign
watched from the town hall window
on the third day he came down

What are you feeding them? he asked sternly
Nothing, said the man
Then why do they come?

And the old man smiled
The bread, that was for you
so you'd have something to forbid

They never came here for bread
they came here for me
and against that
you have no paragraph yet
