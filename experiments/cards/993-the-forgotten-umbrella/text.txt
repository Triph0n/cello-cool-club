The Forgotten Umbrella
(engine card 993, poem/poster #002)

Mr. Smith bought himself an umbrella
a solid black umbrella
so he would never get wet
and the very first Tuesday
he left it on the train to Oxford

The umbrella travelled on
in Oxford a student took it
and under it she kissed a boy
she was not supposed to kiss
and left it in a café

In the café a violinist took it
and played beneath it at the station
when rain fell on his sheet music
then he lent it to a bride
whose veil was catching the rain
and the bride left it in a taxi
and the taxi drove it all the way to the sea

And so the umbrella saw Oxford
a kiss that wasn't allowed to be
notes washed out by rain
a wedding
and the sea

Mr. Smith bought a new umbrella
an even more solid one
and so as not to lose it again
he kept it at home
and when it rained
he stayed home with it

The umbrella saw the world
because it got lost
Mr. Smith saw nothing
because he was afraid of getting lost
