Nine I · 5/9 · The Celestial Household

Jupiter, the Stormy King

After the red soldier and his silence of rust

I am the king, the giant, the big gentleman

I am not made of earth nor of regrets

I am made of gas, of anger, and of solitude

I have no ground to place my feet on

My only land is my own immense weight

I am a painter who paints only with storms

My colors are ocher, brick, and old gold

My brushes are winds of a thousand kilometers per hour

I paint ribbons of clouds that never dry

And in the middle of it all, I have my masterpiece

My great red wound

A cyclone's eye open for centuries

That stares into the void without ever blinking

It is the signature of my melancholy

I have a large family of little moons

My court of silent and faithful servants

They dance around me to try to calm me

They polish my great ball of gas with their little light

They do not understand

That you cannot calm a storm

That is inside of yourself

I am the big protective brother of the family

The goalkeeper of the solar system

With my immense body, I catch the stray stones

The drunken comets that would like to do harm

To my fragile little sisters, Earth or Venus

It is a thankless and solitary job

They are too busy with their little lives

To see the dangers I divert for them

So I turn heavily in the dark

The beating heart of the great celestial clockwork

A king without a castle, a god without prayers

A father who protects his children from afar

And who can only show them

His tormented and magnificent face

I am the great storm that will never end.
