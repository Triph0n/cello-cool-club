Nine I · 4/9 · The Celestial Household

Mars, the Old Soldier

After my sister Earth, noisy and full of life,

I am the fourth, the tired soldier

They call me the God of War

A ridiculous old name for an old man like me

My great wars have been over for millions of years

My armor is covered in rust

And that rust is my only color

I remember a time when I was blue

A time when I wore rivers on my face

Tears of clear water that flowed in my great canyons

Perhaps I remember the taste of rain

And the murmur of a wind that was not made of dust

That was before the great drought

Before the great silence

Today my kingdom is a red desert

My mountains are volcanoes asleep forever

My army is a storm of fine sand

That marches with no purpose and no enemy

My only treasures are two small pieces of ice

At my poles like two cold medals

I am the king of an immense silence

The guardian of a dead world

Sometimes I receive visitors

Little metal insects that my sister Earth sends

They roll slowly over my skin

They take pictures of my old scars

They search for signs of life with their little glass eyes

Like children searching for treasure in an empty attic

I let them be

I am too old for anger

Their curiosity is a small, touching thing

In my great solitude

I look at them and I look at my sister in the distance

That blue and white marble so full of noise and fury

She does not yet know

How lucky she is to have water to cry with

I have nothing left but red dust

So I wrap myself in my thin, cold atmosphere

Like in an old military blanket

And I wait

I don't know for what

I just wait.
