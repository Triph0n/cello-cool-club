Nine I · 1/9 · The Celestial Household

Mercury, the Hurried Messenger

I am the first the closest

The little messenger of the great king Sun

I run I run I never stop

My race is my prayer and my madness

I carry an urgent message

But I have no mouth to speak it

And there is no one to hear it

I have two faces like a liar or a lover

One face turned toward him

Burned with love and with light

A land of molten rock and of despair

Where silence boils under a sky of fire

It is the face of the eternal "yes"

My other face is turned toward the void

Frozen by solitude and by night

A plain of black ice and absolute silence

Where the stars are cold pinpricks

It is the face of "never"

And I live ceaselessly between these two extremes

Between the kiss that burns and the absence that freezes

The other planets watch me from afar

They turn slowly in their queenly gowns

They see me as a little fool a nervous spark

They do not understand my haste and my devotion

They do not know what it is

To love a fire that devours you

On my skin I have scars

The memories of an old war of pebbles

Each crater is a letter of an unknown alphabet

That tells the story of my terrible birth

My message is written on my face

But no one knows how to read it

So I run

With one side of my body on fire

And the other of ice

The first word in the great king's sentence

The first servant and the first prisoner

I am the spinning top that the Sun has launched

And that will never stop turning.
