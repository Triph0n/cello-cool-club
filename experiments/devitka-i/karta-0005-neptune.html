Nine I · 8/9 · The Celestial Household

Neptune, the Lighthouse Keeper

After the rebel lying on his side

I am the last, the distant, the forgotten

The final blue period at the end of the Sun's sentence

They gave me the name of the god of the sea

What a cruel irony

I who reign over a frozen ocean

My kingdom is a world of eternal twilight

Where the Sun's light is just one star among others

A cold and distant pinhead

My color is the deepest blue

Not the blue of life or of hope

But the blue of a deep bruise on the soul

The blue of the great void

I am the king of the wildest winds

Of drafts of two thousand kilometers per hour

My invisible and howling subjects

They are the only music in my icy silence

They tell stories of darkness and speed

The only ones who know the secrets of my surface

I once had a great dark eye

A storm to keep me company

But even it has abandoned me

It dissolved into my blue atmosphere

Leaving me even more alone

With my memories of whirlpools

My job is to be the lighthouse keeper

The last one before the great night between the stars

I keep my blue lantern lit

Not to guide ships, for there are none

But simply to say

"Here our world ends"

"Here infinity begins"

I look at my brothers and sisters

Little lights so distant

They could be memories

I am the king in exile, the guardian of the border

The god of an ocean that no longer remembers water

And I listen to the song of my furious winds

The only proof that I am not

Just a cold and blue dream.
