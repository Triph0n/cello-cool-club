Nine I · 6/9 · The Celestial Household

Saturn, the Sad Ballerina

After my brother king Jupiter

And his great uproar of storms

I am the queen of silence

The great melancholy lady

I am not heavy I am light

They say I could float on the ocean

What a funny idea

I only dream of stopping floating

Around my waist I have my adornment and my prison

My beautiful rings of ice and dust

The other planets look at them with envy

They do not know what it is

To eternally wear the debris of a broken love

The frozen memory of a lost moon

I am a sad ballerina in a giant music box

I turn slowly on my axis

An endless pirouette without applause

My dress is a hoop of diamonds and frost

A silent carousel for the ghosts of light

A great necklace of frozen tears

That will never fall

My little moons are my only spectators

They watch me turn with faithful eyes

But they are far away

On the other side of my beautiful barrier of ice

I am the most beautiful perhaps

But I am the loneliest

Beauty is a cold thing that creates distance

I do not have the anger of Mars nor the life of Earth

I do not have the fire of the Sun nor the poison of Venus

I have only my cold and perfect beauty

And my eternal circular motion

I am the great white sigh of the solar system

The forgotten pearl at the bottom of the night's jewelry box

The dream of a prisoner

Who has decorated the walls of his cell.
