Nine I · 7/9 · The Celestial Household

Uranus, the Silent Rebel

After the sad dance of Saturn

I am the seventh, the strange one, the eccentric

All the other planets stand up straight

Like well-behaved soldiers or obedient spinning tops

I have lain down on my side

It was not an accident, a cosmic shoving match

It was a choice

I had had enough of looking up and down

I wanted to look at the world from the side

To see if the universe was different from that point of view

It is my small, silent revolution

My act of laziness and of disobedience

I do not spin, I roll

Like a child in the grass or a barrel of blue wine

My kingdom is of a pale and quiet color

A blue-green of a frozen lagoon

I do not have the great angers of Jupiter

Nor the ostentatious jewels of Saturn

My beauty is discreet and uneventful

I am a great calm eye floating in the dark

My few rings are so thin

They look like forgotten spider threads

And my moons have names from poetry

Miranda, Titania, Oberon

They are characters from old, sad plays

My only companions in the great cold

They understand my melancholy

From afar they look at me with curiosity

The tilted planet, the anomaly of the system

They wonder if I am sick or tired

They do not understand that rest can be a form of protest

That silence can be a strong word

So I continue my long and slow roll

In the distant suburbs of the Sun's kingdom

I ask nothing of anyone

I am at peace with my own strangeness

And I simply prove

That there is more than one way to dance

Around the great fire of life.
