Nine I · 2/9 · The Celestial Household

Venus, the Veiled Beauty

After the little mad messenger

I am the second

The most beautiful, they say

The shepherd's star, the evening star

The lovers on Earth look at me and sigh

They give me the name of their most beautiful goddess

Venus

They do not know

They see only my great cloak of clouds

My gown of white and golden silk

They do not see what lies beneath

My true face

If a poet came here seeking love

He would find an oven, a hell

My air is not air, it is a poison that crushes

My rain is not water, it is acid that burns

My kisses are bites of fire

I am the great queen of solitude

My kingdom is a desert of red rocks

Where volcanoes shout their stony anger

There is not a single bird, not a single flower

The silence here is not the silence of peace

It is the heavy silence of death

Nothing can live beneath my beautiful poisoned gown

They call me Earth's twin sister

A sister who went wrong

A warning, a terrible mirror

Of what love can become

When it locks itself in its own heat

And forgets to breathe

So I turn slowly on my axis

In my magnificent prison of gas

I am the brightest in the night sky

All eyes turn to me

And no one sees my sorrow

I am the promise of an impossible beauty

The love that one can only admire from very, very far away.
