Nine I · 9/9 · The Celestial Household

The Sun, the Lonely King

At the center of everything there is me

The old solitary king dressed in fire

I have no throne no crown

Just my own burning heart

Which serves as my palace and my prison

Every morning I rise with great effort

I push away the heavy blanket of the night

And I begin my eternal work

I am the great alarm clock of the world

I shout in the ear of the roosters

And I paint a fine line of gold under the door of the sleepers

I am the great painter without a brush

Without me the flower is a gray shape

The ocean a large black spot

The face of the beloved a simple absence

I give the red to the cherry

The green to the meadow

And the changing blue of the eyes that look at the sky

I am the patient baker

Who makes the wheat dough rise in the fields

I am the great clothes dryer of the world

Who dries the shirts of workers and the dresses of lovers

I am the companion of the old on public benches

And the warm rug for the cat at the window

I am the excuse for a lunch on the terrace

But I am terribly alone

I see the children playing in the parks

The couples holding hands

The friends sharing a bottle of wine

I warm everyone

But no one can approach me

I give all my warmth

And I remain in my great silence of fire

Sometimes my work exhausts me

My heat becomes an anger

I beat down on the city like a hammer

I melt the asphalt and crack the earth

People complain and hide from me

They forget that without me they would be nothing

In the evening I am tired

I descend slowly behind the horizon

Like a worker going home after a long day

I leave the place to my sister the moon

Pale and discreet

Who comes to console the world from my great light

And to watch over the sleep of my small distant kingdom.
