The Café's Last Customer

The waiter puts the chairs on the tables

It makes a sound of climbing skeletons

The coffee machine sighs one last time

Outside night has eaten everything

There remains a man alone at a table

He looks at the bottom of his glass

As if he were looking for an answer there

He thinks of nothing

He is just there

In the silence that settles in

In the smell of cold coffee and cigarettes

He is the last survivor of the day

The guardian of a moment that no longer exists

The waiter waits

He says nothing

He knows you do not disturb

Someone who is becoming

A memory.
